All Nations Church of Jesus Christ in your pocket

Tap through the phone to see how members and visitors could join, pray, read, give and stay close to their pastors from one app. Content here is sample content, and the real details would come from the church.

Jump to a screen

What the full app could include

  • Sign up with email and verification
  • Connect with Pastor Mechack and ministry leaders
  • Daily Bible verse and devotionals
  • Events, reminders and registration
  • Sermon library with audio and video
  • Giving with receipts and history
  • Private prayer requests
  • Admin dashboard for church staff

Items marked Sample are placeholders because service times, ministry names, leaders and sermons have not been provided yet. Bible verses are from the King James Version. No payments are taken, no email is sent, and nothing you type is saved.
